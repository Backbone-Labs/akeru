# Backbone branding

The Backbone symbol and wordmark embedded in `platform/catalog/index.html` are
existing Backbone brand artwork, used for this Backbone feature. They are not
new Akeru logos and are not covered by the repository's MIT software license.
Backbone retains its trademark and artwork rights. The software license does not
grant permission to use Backbone branding to imply endorsement of other products.

The catalog uses Backbone orange (`#F77041`) with black, white and neutral gray
surfaces. The same palette applies to shell controls and the original local demo.
Logo geometry is preserved; the SVG symbols share the surrounding foreground color.
